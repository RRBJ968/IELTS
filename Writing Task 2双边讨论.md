# 双边讨论类大作文（Discussion Essay）

## 1. Introduction — IPO

- **I — Paraphrase：** 改写题目
- **P — Both Views：** 引出双方观点
- **O — Own Opinion：** 表达个人观点

**Template:**

There is ongoing debate over…

Although/While some people think that…, others believe that…

From my perspective, I agree with the latter view, as…

---

## 2. Body Paragraph 1 — PEEL（反方观点）

- **P — Point：** 反方观点
- **E — Explain：** 解释
- **E — Example：** 举例
- **L — Link：** 小结

**Template:**

**Point:**  
Opponents of… often cite…

**Explain:**  
It is true that…

**Example:**  
For example, …

Furthermore, …

**Link:**  
Therefore, …

---

## 3. Body Paragraph 2 — PEEEL（己方观点）

- **P — Point：** 己方观点
- **E — Explain：** 解释原因
- **E — Example 1：** 举例
- **E — Example 2：** 补充例子
- **L — Link：** 小结

**Template:**

**Point:**  
Nevertheless, such issues may be effectively resolved by…, and in my opinion, …

**Explain:**  
This is mainly because …

**Example 1:**  
A prime example is that …

**Example 2:**  
Likewise, …

**Link:**  
Therefore, …
